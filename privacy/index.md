This policy explains how **Tapesync** (a trading name of **Jack & Julia
Liberation Technology AB**, org.nr 559411-9165, Odengatan 13, 114 24
Stockholm, Sweden, "we", "us") handles personal data about the people who
use the Tapesync Service: the web app, the mobile apps and the phone-in
service.

We are the controller for the data described here. Questions and requests go
to **info@tapesync.com**.

## 1. What this policy covers, and what it doesn't

**It covers your account:** who you are, how you log in, and the technical
data created when you use Tapesync.

**It does not cover the content of your recordings.** For recordings,
transcripts and call data, the customer who made the recording is the
controller and we only process them on their behalf. That is governed by the
Data Processing Agreement in Annex A of our Terms of Service.

**If you were recorded as a guest** (you joined a call by link, by phone or in
the app, without an account), the person or organisation who invited you is
responsible for the recording. Contact them with questions, or to ask for the
recording to be deleted. If you can't reach them, write to us at
info@tapesync.com and we will pass your request on.

## 4. What we don't do

- **No analytics or tracking.** We don't use analytics tools, tracking pixels
  or advertising networks, in the web app or in the mobile apps.
- **No selling or renting** of personal data, to anyone.
- **No marketing** from the Service. If we ever want to send you marketing,
  we'll ask first.
- **No AI training** on your data or your recordings.
- **No automated decisions** with legal or similarly significant effects on
  you.

## 5. Cookies and storage on your device

We only use what the Service needs to work, so we don't ask for cookie
consent.

- **Cookies:** session cookies that keep you logged in
  (`tapesync_main_sessionid`, `tapesync_calls_sessionid`) and a security
  cookie that protects forms against forgery.
- **Browser storage:** your theme setting, the microphone and other audio
  devices you've chosen, and display information such as your name and
  whether you're logged in. These never leave your device, and they don't
  contain your password or login credentials.

**In the mobile apps**, Tapesync asks for these permissions:

- **Microphone**, to record calls. What it records is recording content (see
  section 1).
- **Camera**, only to scan QR codes for joining a call. Images are handled on
  your device and are never stored or sent anywhere.
- **Notifications**, to show that a recording is in progress.

If you downloaded the app from the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google
also collect data about the download under their own privacy policies.

## 10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
- have it **corrected** if it's wrong;
- have it **deleted**;
- **restrict** how we use it while a complaint is being resolved;
- get the data you gave us in a machine-readable format (**portability**);
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interest.

Self-service tools for this don't exist yet, so email info@tapesync.com from
the address on your account. We'll answer within one month.

If you think we're handling your data wrongly, you can complain to the
Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY,
www.imy.se), or to the data protection authority where you live or work. We
would appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.

## 11. Children

Tapesync accounts are for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly hold
account data about children.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we will email you at
least **14 days before** the change takes effect.
