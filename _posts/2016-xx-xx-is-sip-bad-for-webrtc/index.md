>    I've been experimenting on this for some time now. In August I presented a
>    solution for WebRTC/SIP interoperation, based on Kamailio andFreeSWITCH, at
>    ClueCon. In that case signalling was accomplished with SIP on both sides (using
>    the JsSIP library on the clients); unsurprisingly, after using verto, SIP on
>    the web browser client side looks even more redundant, over-complex, but most
>    of all with a steeper learning curve for web developers, and this is becoming
>    every day a stronger selling point for new signalling protocols for WebRTC
>    applications.
