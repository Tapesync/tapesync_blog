TapeSync is the premier call recording solution for documentary radio
producers.  It consists of a desktop client (Mac and Windows) and a mobile
client (iOS and Android). From either client you can make calls to any phone.
Your own end is captured in high fidelity (44 kHz, 16 bit uncompressed wav) and
the other end in 8 kHz. If there's time to have the other party install a
client both ends will be captured in high fidelity.

## How do I get it?

TapeSync will launch in 2017, starting with the desktop client. I'll be
running beta tests with early adopters before launch. Subscribe to the
mailing list and I'll ping you when the betas start and whenever there's
important news.
